The Drop
Index

Every named location Epic has put on the Battle Royale map, graded on the loot-rarity ladder — gold down to green. Each one is judged on the four things that actually decide a drop spot: how much loot it held, where it sat in the circle, how you got out of it, and whether it handed you something nobody else had.

    How the grades work

    S defined its season — loot, position, rotation and a unique reward all at once. A was a great drop with one weak leg. B was a solid, fair landing spot. C was survivable but you were behind. D punished you for landing there.

    On the chest numbers

    Chest counts are the community-counted spawn pools, not guarantees — every chest is a spawn chance, and Epic re-tuned them constantly inside a season. Treat them as size, not as promises. Where a count was never reliably documented the field reads a dash.

    Where the D tier went

    Only four locations grade out at D, and every one of them is from the original island. After Chapter 1, Epic stopped shipping dead corners: vehicles, redeploy and bar-bought loadouts mean a weak drop no longer loses you the game. The floor rose, so the modern map is mostly B.

    Coverage and gaps

    Named locations only, plus the handful of landmarks that mattered enough to fight over. Chapter 6 Season 3 onward is thinner on chest and grid detail than the older chapters, so those entries lean on layout and mechanics instead of numbers.
